Every early vote in Iowa is an absentee ballot; the 20-day window runs from 14 October to 2 November. Polls through 22 October. Page refreshed 26 October, 2:32 a.m. ET.

Iowa early vote: who is turning out

Iowans have cast 207,858 ballots so far, 24.4% more than at the same point in 2022 and 17% of 2022's entire vote. Registered Democrats minus Republicans: D+18.9 of ballots, against D+23.8 at this stage in 2022. Combining the polls with the early vote, the U.S. Senate race leans slightly toward Hinson and the governor's race leans toward Sand.
