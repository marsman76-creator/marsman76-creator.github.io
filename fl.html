Florida early vote: who is turning out

Floridians have cast 2.60 million ballots so far, 33% of 2022's entire vote. Registered Democrats minus Republicans: D+0.1 of ballots, against R+3.5 at this stage in 2022. Combining the polls with the early vote, the U.S. Senate race leans toward Moody and the governor's race leans toward Donalds.
