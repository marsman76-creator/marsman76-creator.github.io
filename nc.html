North Carolina early vote: who is turning out

North Carolinians have cast 1.54 million ballots so far, 36.6% more than at the same point in 2022 and 41% of 2022's entire vote. Black voters are 21.9% of ballots, against 18.5% at this stage in 2022. Combining the polls with the early vote, the U.S. Senate race strongly favors Cooper.
