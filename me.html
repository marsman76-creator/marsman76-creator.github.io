Maine Early Vote Tracker

Early-vote figures from the Maine Secretary of State's absentee voter file of 23 October (2:57 p.m.), as tallied by Call the Map, covering absentee voting from Monday 5 October through Thursday 22 October, statewide and by congressional district. Absentee ballots received and accepted by town and city clerks, whether cast in person at the clerk's office or returned by mail or drop box. Maine has no separate early voting: any voter may vote absentee, no excuse needed, through Thursday 29 October, and mailed ballots count if they reach the town by 8 p.m. on Election Day. Polls through 23 October. Page refreshed 26 October, 2:26 a.m. ET.

Maine absentee vote: who is turning out

Mainers have cast 174,765 ballots so far, 40% more than on the same day of voting in 2022 and 26% of 2022's entire vote. Registered Democrats minus Republicans: D+29.3 of ballots, against D+29.9 at this stage in 2022. Combining the polls with the early vote, the U.S. Senate race leans slightly toward Jackson and the governor's race strongly favors Pingree.
