Mississippi absentee vote: who is turning out

Mississippians have cast 29,647 ballots so far, 191.1% more than at the same point in 2022 and 64% of all the absentee ballots received before Election Day in 2022. On the polls, the U.S. Senate race leans toward Hyde-Smith.
