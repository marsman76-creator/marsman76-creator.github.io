Michigan early vote: who is turning out

Michiganders have cast 986,189 ballots so far, 2.3% more two weeks before Election Day than at the same point in 2022 and 22% of 2022's entire vote. On the polls, the U.S. Senate race leans slightly toward El-Sayed and the governor's race strongly favors Benson.
