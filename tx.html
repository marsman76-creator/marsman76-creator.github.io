Texas early vote: who is turning out

Texans have cast 3.72 million ballots so far, with 55% more in-person votes than at the same point in 2022 and 46% of 2022's entire vote. On the polls, the U.S. Senate race leans slightly toward Talarico and the governor's race leans slightly toward Abbott.
