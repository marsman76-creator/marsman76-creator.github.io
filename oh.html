Ohio early vote: who is turning out

Ohioans have cast 1.00 million ballots so far, 30% more through 20 October than at the same point in 2022, by AP's count and 24% of 2022's entire vote. On the polls, the U.S. Senate race leans toward Brown and the governor's race leans toward Acton.
