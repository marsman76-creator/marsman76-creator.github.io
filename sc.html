South Carolina early vote: who is turning out

South Carolinians have cast 501,944 ballots so far, 110% more after four days than in the first four days of 2022 and 29% of 2022's entire vote. On the polls, the U.S. Senate race is a toss-up and the governor's race strongly favors Wilson.
